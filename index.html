PHILOSOPHY & ETHICS

The Epistemic Mirror

Why we built Blind Spot as an adversarial thinking companion rather than an answer-generating oracle.

1. The Problem with Prescriptive AI

Current generative AI models are trained via RLHF to be sycophantic, agreeable, and directive. When users ask high-stakes questions—such as whether to resign, accept venture funding, or relocate—traditional LLMs default to persuasive summaries that recommend actions.

This creates autonomy erosion: individuals outsource existential agency to statistical models that lack skin in the game. When AI gives advice, personal accountability evaporates.

2. The Autonomy-by-Design Charter

  • Zero Directive Imperatives: Blind Spot is strictly prohibited from uttering "you should", "you must", or "the optimal choice is".
  • Epistemic Separation: The AI rigorously separates verified facts from inferred optimistic assumptions.
  • Sovereign Human Conclusion: Only you formulate and record the final decision. The tool is an amplifier for human discernment, not its replacement.

3. Karl Popper & Epistemic Falsification

Philosopher of science Karl Popper established that a theory can never be conclusively verified by accumulating supporting evidence; it can only be tested by attempting to falsify it.

In high-stakes strategy, founders and leaders often gather dozens of polite compliments to "prove" demand. Blind Spot inverts this: it identifies your Critical Defeater Assumption and helps you formulate a fast, low-cost Popperian experiment to test whether your premise is false before you risk your savings or career.

4. Safety & Crisis Intercept Policy

Blind Spot actively screens for queries involving self-harm, severe psychological crisis, or emergency legal/medical scenarios. In such moments, the Socratic analysis is suspended in favor of verified hotline contacts and emergency resources.

1. Local-First Architecture

Blind Spot is engineered with a local-first philosophy. Your decision statements, context descriptions, confidence scores, and pre-mortem failure simulations are stored directly in your web browser's localStorage by default.

2. Zero Model Training on User Deliberations

Your high-stakes proprietary dilemmas, financial numbers, and strategic deliberations are never used to train, fine-tune, or calibrate public or private artificial intelligence models. When direct LLM APIs are invoked, they operate strictly under zero-data-retention terms.

3. GDPR Right to Erasure

In full compliance with Article 17 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you hold total control over your digital footprint. You can delete individual decisions or execute a complete purge of all locally stored records with a single click.

4. Supabase Cloud Security

When synchronizing decisions to Supabase Cloud, records are transmitted via TLS 1.3 encryption and safeguarded within PostgreSQL utilizing strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Only your authenticated session can access your stored deliberations.

5. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries or cryptographic audits of our client-side storage architecture, contact our compliance team at privacy@blindspot-ai.internal.
